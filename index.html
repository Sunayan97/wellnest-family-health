Sleep reset

A few realistic ideas for the whole household.

  • Keep a broadly consistent wake-up time.
  • Make the last 30 minutes before bed quieter.
  • Use the bedroom mainly for sleep and rest.

Movement menu

Pick the smallest activity everyone can repeat.

  • Take a 10-minute walk after a meal.
  • Set a family stretch timer between study sessions.
  • Turn chores into shared movement.

Everyday nutrition

Keep the focus on variety, not perfection.

  • Add a fruit or vegetable to one meal.
  • Keep water visible and easy to reach.
  • Plan one simple meal together.

Mindful pauses

Small resets can help busy family days feel more manageable.

  • Try five slow breaths before a difficult task.
  • Take a short screen-free break.
  • Talk about energy and mood without judgment.
